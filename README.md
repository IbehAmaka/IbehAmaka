### 🧠 What I think about

- ESG data should be traceable end-to-end — if you cannot generate the evidence chain for any disclosed figure in one click, the audit trail is not finished
- Sustainability reporting becomes strategic only when other departments — finance, operations, HR — can access and act on the same data without going through the sustainability team as a bottleneck
- A missing safety report should be treated with higher urgency than a missing carbon data point — the human stakes are different
- Automation reduces manual burden but does not replace methodology judgement — someone still has to know why the number is right, not just that the system produced it
- The gap between limited assurance and reasonable assurance is almost entirely about data architecture decisions made years earlier
